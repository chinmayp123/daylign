Your device's own Reduce Motion setting is already respected — these are extra overrides.

Haptics work on Android. On iPhone the web has no vibration API, so Daylign borrows the tick from Apple's own switch control — it may do nothing on your device, in which case everything still behaves exactly as before.

Your data

Backup your data

Your data syncs to the cloud automatically, but the cloud keeps no history. Download a backup regularly so you always have a copy you control. Save it somewhere safe (Files, iCloud, email).

Import from a spreadsheet

UAT findings and bug lists usually start life in a sheet, where nothing moves through anything. Paste the rows and they become tasks on the board. Columns are matched by their headings — Title, Status, Due, Reported by — and you see exactly what will be created before anything is written.

Recent errors

When something says “that action may not have saved”, the reason lands here. There is no console on an installed app, so this is the only place to see it.

How the app is being used

A read-only look at what each profile is actually logging — useful for working out which parts of Daylign are worth improving. This never loads anyone else's data into the app, so it cannot change their numbers.

Restore from a backup

This replaces everything — on this device and in the cloud (all your devices) — with the contents of a backup file. Only use it to recover lost data. Restoring an older file will delete anything newer. A safety copy of your current data is downloaded first, and you'll be asked to type RESTORE to confirm.
