Your day, on one line.

A new direction for Daylign. The Today screen stops being a dashboard of tiles and becomes the day itself: one time line where meals, water, habits, meetings, workouts and tasks sit at the moment they happen, with your numbers riding at "now".

Time is the layout

Past above, future below, a blue line at now. Tasks with no time wait in a tray on the line. Nothing is a floating card.

Numbers stay pinned

The dark block at the top follows you as you scroll, so your data is always visible. Tap it for all eight numbers.

Colour means something

Every kind of event has its own colour: sleep, food, water, movement, meetings and habits. The line runs from night into day. Blue is kept for now and for buttons.

Type with a voice

Bricolage Grotesque for the day and the brief, JetBrains Mono for every number, Geist for the rest.

What's different from today's app

  • No tiles. The eight health numbers become one pinned "now" block: calories large, then protein, water and steps with tick scales, then burn, exercise, sleep and weight on one line.
  • The brief is one big sentence in plain words, with its actions right under it (these replace the reminder cards).
  • Meals, water, sleep and habits are logged events on the line, so Today and Diet read the same way.
  • Timed tasks and meetings sit at their time; the Anytime tray and the triage nudge sit at "now"; due-soon tasks close the page.
  • The bottom bar floats and puts the + button in the middle: task, food, water, weight, workout, event or voice.
  • Nothing in the feature list is lost. Every item keeps the home mapped in the inventory (weigh-in, goals, theme and categories each get one home).
